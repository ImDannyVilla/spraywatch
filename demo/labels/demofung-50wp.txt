AGRICULTURAL USE REQUIREMENTS
Use this product only in accordance with its labeling and with the Worker
Protection Standard, 40 CFR part 170.

DIRECTIONS FOR USE

GRAPES
Disease: Powdery mildew, Botrytis bunch rot
Rate: 1 to 1.5 lb product per acre
Apply at 10 to 14 day intervals beginning at bud break.
RESTRICTIONS:
- Do not apply within 7 days of harvest.
- Do not apply more than 6 lb product per acre per season.

STRAWBERRIES
Disease: Gray mold, Anthracnose
Rate: 1 lb product per acre
Apply at 7 day intervals during bloom.
RESTRICTIONS:
- May be applied up to the day of harvest.
- Do not make more than 5 applications per season.
